The story behind Sparsh K. Enterprises

"What if we could craft a studio that inspires creativity, fosters connection, and produces exceptional sound?"

Sparsh K. Enterprises started with that question. We wanted a place where voice artists, directors and musicians could leave the noise of the day outside, sit down in front of a microphone and simply do their best work.

Every part of the studio was designed by our own team, from the acoustic treatment in the booths to the console in the control room. Today we are a localization house for film, television and streaming, handling dubbing, voice-over, Foley, sound design and mixing under one roof, and a recording space for musicians of every level.

Our values

These guide every session we run, every project we take on and every person we work with.

Creativity

We encourage experimentation and artistic expression, in the booth and at the console.

Community

We build a sense of belonging among artists, staff and partners.

Excellence

We strive for the highest quality in our results and in the equipment we use.

Authenticity

We preserve each artist's own voice and style rather than flattening it.

Collaboration

We work closely with artists, producers, directors and musicians at every step.

Respect

We treat people and the creative process with kindness and empathy.

Growth

We keep up with new technology, techniques and trends so your work never dates.

Commitment

We are dedicated to your sound. We aim to exceed expectations and support your creative vision from the first take to delivery.

Innovation

We adopt new technologies and ideas early, and the studio itself is designed to encourage experimentation.

Ownership

Your music, your vision and your voice stay yours. We support artists rather than direct them.
