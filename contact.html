General questions

Quick answers to the things people ask before their first session.

What services does Sparsh K. Enterprises offer?

Dubbing and voice-over recording, Foley and sound design, music recording, mixing in stereo and Atmos, mastering and audio editing. Our engineers can also assist with production on your project.

Where is the studio located?

In Andheri West, Mumbai. The full address and map are above.

How do I book a session?

Use the form on this page, or call or email us to schedule. We will confirm the room, time and engineer.

What is the minimum booking time?

Two hours.

What equipment does the studio use?

Large-diaphragm condenser microphones, Focusrite ISA One and Audient preamps, an Avid C24 console and industry-standard software including Pro Tools. See the studio pages for details.

Do you offer audio engineering and production services?

Yes. Our experienced engineers can record, edit, mix and master your project, and we can provide dubbing artists and voice-over talent.

Do you offer package deals or discounts?

Yes, custom package options are available on request. Tell us about the project and we will put together a quote.

Dubbing, voice-over, Foley, sound design, music recording and mixing for film, TV and streaming localization.
