  • Industry-leading microphones and preamps
  • Soundproof, acoustically treated recording booths

  • Post-production editing and mixing included on request

Sound mixing

Our skilled engineers balance, blend and enhance your tracks to achieve a polished, professional sound. Mixing takes place on a dedicated console in an accurately tuned control room.

  • Multitrack mixing for music, podcasts and voice-overs
  • Stereo, 5.1, 7.1 and Dolby Atmos mixing
  • Noise reduction and audio restoration
  • EQ, compression and effects processing
  • Mixing across every genre and format

Track mastering

Mastering is the final step of post-production. It takes a finished mix and prepares it for distribution, making sure it sounds right everywhere it will be played.

  • Loudness that meets streaming and broadcast standards
  • Clarity and consistency across the whole release
  • Translation across headphones, speakers, cars and phones
  • Delivery for streaming, CD, radio, film and live playback

Audio editing

Audio editing is the craft of shaping recorded sound into exactly what the project needs. We handle corrective, creative and mix-preparation editing for every kind of production.

  • Cutting, arranging and comping takes
  • Timing, pitch and tone correction
  • Dialogue cleanup and sync to picture
  • Pro Tools, Cubase Pro and Logic Pro, with current plug-ins and hardware
  • Music production, film, TV, games, podcasts and voice-overs

Live recording

Live recording captures audio in real time, preserving the energy of the performance and the room. We record concerts, theatre, public speaking and events such as weddings and corporate functions.

  • Multitrack, stereo and binaural recording
  • Dynamic, condenser and ribbon microphones
  • Interfaces, mixing consoles and portable recorders
  • Handling of room acoustics, noise and redundancy on site
  • Editing, mixing and mastering after the event
