Studio 1 - Mixing and dubbing

Our flagship space, designed for expansive soundscapes and bold creativity, featuring top-tier equipment and versatile acoustics.

About this studio

Studio 1 is the heart of the facility: a tuned control room built around an Avid C24 console, with a large-format screen for picture work and a dedicated voice booth behind glass. It is where our dubbing projects are directed and mixed, and where music mixes and masters are finished.

The room is sized for a director, a client and a small crew to sit comfortably through long sessions, and the monitoring is set up for stereo, surround and Atmos deliverables.
