Studio 2 - Voice and music

An intimate and focused environment, perfect for solo artists, vocal recordings and precise production, with a curated selection of high-end gear.

About this studio

Studio 2 pairs a warm, acoustically treated voice booth with a compact control room. It is our go-to room for dubbing artists, voice-over sessions, podcast recording and vocal tracking, where a focused space and a great microphone chain matter more than a big console.

The booth is treated with pyramid foam and diffusion, the sightline to the engineer is clear, and talkback and headphone mixes are set up so artists can settle in and perform.
